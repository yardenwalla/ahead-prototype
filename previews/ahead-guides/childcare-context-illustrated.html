Your childcare guide · Care started

How can you tell it’s going well?

Look beyond the goodbye. What matters is how Maya is cared for throughout the day.

This stage · Settling in and building trust
✳ Worth knowing now
Tears at drop-off don’t tell the whole story.

Some babies protest a goodbye, especially as separation anxiety emerges. Ask how she settles afterward and what helps. Notice the pattern over time.

What should you look for?

✓Reassuring signs

Warm responses, honest updates, and a baby who can be comforted by their caregiver.

!Worth addressing

Unsafe sleep, unexplained injuries, or concerns that are repeatedly dismissed.
